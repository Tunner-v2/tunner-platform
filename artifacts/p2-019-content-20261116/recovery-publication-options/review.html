B: minimum technical design to review

  1. Ownership and isolation: a new Content-owned transactional reader builds a separate read-only PresentationContentDbContext over the borrowed physical connection and DbTransaction. Await operations sequentially; fully materialize and dispose readers. It never calls Content SaveChanges or commits/disposes the owner transaction. EF supports shared transactions only with the same connection and transaction; Npgsql does not support nested transactions on that connection. [Microsoft, Npgsql]
  2. Publication coherence: existing resolution uses multiple SELECTs. PostgreSQL Read Committed permits a different snapshot per statement; sharing a transaction alone does not pin one bundle. Reuse Content's existing per-scope/key/variant publication-lock identities in canonical order for all relevant configured fallback candidates, holding them through Identity completion. Existing publish/rollback already participate in those identities. Require current profile qualification again after awaited reads; capture exact selected publication bounds. A row lock on an old immutable publication alone does not prevent a successor INSERT. [PostgreSQL isolation, advisory locks]
  3. Native frontier: split Recovery's awaited publication preparation from its local tracked-state validation. After the last awaited read, recapture time and recheck session, proof, policy, exact original impact/admission, publication digest/version/key, and all effective bounds. The witness deadline must include the earliest already-committed future publication, supersession, fallback-resolution, profile or binding transition, even when the selected publication has no expiry; locks do not stop time-triggered transitions. Preserve private delivery ownership until successful COMMIT and transaction disposal. Add a Recovery-only local bounds check after the awaited SaveChanges before COMMIT; do not reuse the pre-save EntityState assertions after EF accepts tracked changes. Any failure/cancellation rolls back issuance, retirement, proof consumption, audit/outbox and disposes the private delivery.
